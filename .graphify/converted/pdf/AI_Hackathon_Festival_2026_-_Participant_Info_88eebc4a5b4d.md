Feel free to approach the mentors Suggested Problem: Food Waste Challenge The Problem • An estimated 32% of food is wasted globally (13% along supply chains, 19% by consumers). • Food waste breaks down in landfills to produce methane, a potent greenhouse gas contributing to climate change. • In NZ, 1 in 5 children live in food-insecure households, and over 10% of New Zealanders face food insecurity. • Wasted food also wastes land, fresh water (25% globally), energy, packaging, and transport emissions. AI Opportunities & Ideas • Predictive Analytics: Forecast when and where surplus or waste is likely to occur. • Demand Forecasting: Improve inventory management and supply chain planning to prevent overproduction. • Redistribution Logistics: Optimize food rescue operations to connect surplus food with communities in need. • Impact Measurement: Track environmental, social, and economic benefits of food redistribution. Suggested Problem: Facilities Helpdesk AI Agent The Problem • Enterprise maintenance requests rely heavily on manual steps, separate process knowledge, and email follow-up. • Desktop-only legacy tools prevent staff away from workstations from easily raising requests. • Lack of real-time progress visibility leads to unnecessary email queries and wasted coordination time. • Critical hazard escalations and priority assessments are not surfaced contextually when logging issues. AI Opportunities & Ideas • Natural Language Intake: Build conversational experiences to report maintenance needs using plain language. • Intelligent Classification: Apply priority models (P0-P4) automatically based on request details. • Contextual Escalation: Automatically detect health & safety risks and surface emergency contacts instantly. • Automated Updates: Proactively notify requesters on progress, eliminating manual email coordination. Suggested Problem: Accessible Peer Support Platforms The Problem • Disabled people and their whānau often face barriers finding advocacy networks, mentors, and local support resources. • Access to training and peer connection is frequently fragmented, making self-determination difficult. • Services must empower individuals according to 'Enabling Good Lives' principles, placing disabled voices in control. • Standard digital interfaces are often not accessible to those with diverse cognitive, visual, or physical impairments. AI Opportunities & Ideas • Intelligent Mentor Connection: Match disabled individuals with peer mentors based on lived experience and goals. • Accessibility Assistant: Design speech, audio, and sign-language translation helpers for training and resource navigation. • Plain Language Summarizer: Convert complex legal rights or service guides into clear, accessible formats. • Inclusive UI Design: Build interfaces that adjust to individual user accessibility requirements dynamically.
